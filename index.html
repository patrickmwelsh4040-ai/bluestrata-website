The Reality

Most SMB Technology Was Not Built From a Plan

It grew one laptop, one cloud app, one workaround, one vendor, and one emergency at a time. That works for a while — until growth, cyber insurance, AI adoption, or a security incident exposes the cracks.

BlueStrata starts with what is worrying you or slowing the team down, traces it to the technical cause, and fixes what matters first. That means knowing what exists, what is exposed, and what is undocumented before anything gets touched.

  • We’re not sure who still has access. Stale accounts, shared passwords, and Microsoft 365 permissions nobody has reviewed.
  • Money moved and nobody approved it. Email impersonation and payment-change fraud with no controls to stop it.
  • We think we have backups, but nobody has tested them. Backups that report success but have never been restored.
  • We don’t know which computers are protected. Inconsistent patching, encryption, or EDR coverage.
  • Only one person knows how it all works. Firewall, VPN, Wi-Fi, and network gear nobody has documented.
  • Our insurer wants proof we can’t produce. Cyber insurance controls that cannot be shown with evidence.
  • Employees are already using AI, and nobody set the rules. AI tools with no policy or guardrails.
  • Technology only gets attention when it breaks. No quarterly roadmap tied to business goals.

Common Questions

Questions Before You Get Started

Do we need to replace our current IT provider?

No. I can take responsibility for one area, finish a project, or support your existing IT person. We agree up front who owns each task and who approves changes.

Can we buy just one service?

Yes. You can start with a single project or a narrowly scoped ongoing service. Broader coverage is optional, and a project never requires an ongoing plan.

What happens on the free discovery call?

It’s a free 30–45 minute remote conversation that starts with questions about your business, not a technical audit: what decisions you’re making without the information you need, what worries you about your technology, and where you’re spending money you can’t explain. Afterward you get a short written recap covering your priorities, what we know, what we don’t, and the recommended next step. There is no obligation to buy.

Do you provide 24/7 or emergency coverage?

No. BlueStrata does not provide 24/7 support or incident response. Evening and weekend windows are agreed in advance, and emergency after-hours work is available by arrangement, generally $200–$300 per hour. If you suspect a breach, call your cyber insurance carrier first, then call me.

What if you’re unavailable?

You work directly with me, the founder. Your documentation, admin accounts, and recovery details belong to you and are kept so another qualified provider could take over.
