More Questions

Common Questions About Working With BlueStrata

Do you work onsite?

Yes. Service is remote-first, with onsite visits across Aurora and the Denver Metro when the job needs it. Onsite work has a two-hour minimum plus travel.

Do we have to use Microsoft 365?

No. Microsoft 365 and Azure are my specialty, but I also support Google Workspace environments, and equipment, network, server, and general support work in mixed environments. Google Workspace work is scoped individually.

Who owns our accounts and documentation?

Your business keeps ownership of its accounts, equipment, and business data. Access uses named accounts and appropriate permissions where supported. Deliverables and handoff responsibilities are documented, and credentials are transferred securely.
